Seminar: Paper Presentation 

AI for Cybersecurity (D7084E/D7041E) 
Note: the seminar is held once. There is no second chance. Read this page before you choose your paper. 

1. What you do 
Your group chooses one published research paper on a topic from this course, or on any application of AI to 
cybersecurity, and presents it to the class. Fifteen groups present on the same day. 

2. Choosing the paper 
• Peer-reviewed, published in a journal or at a conference. Preprints, blog posts and magazine articles are not 
accepted. 
• On a course topic (supervised, semi-supervised or self-supervised learning, explainable AI, belief rule-based 
systems) or on an AI application in cybersecurity (intrusion detection, malware, ransomware, phishing, fraud, 
critical infrastructure). 
• Published 2020 or later, unless you can justify an older paper. Papers cited in the lecture slides may be used. 
• You can present your master thesis research. 
3. Recordings, before the seminar 
Every member records themselves presenting their own part of the talk. 

• Face visible throughout, camera on, with your slides on screen. 
• Three to five minutes per member. One file per member, named Group_NN_Surname.mp4 
• Upload to CANVAS by23:59 on 13 Oct 2026. 
• A member whose recording is missing fails, even if the group presents well. The recordings are how I confirm 
that every member took part in preparing the work ( send one group recode). 
4. The presentation 
• Ten to twelve minutes, then five minutes of questions. Keep to time, I will stop you at twelve minutes. 
• Every member speaks. 
• Ten to fifteen slides: the problem and why it matters, the data, the method, the results, what the paper does 
well and where it is weak, how it connects to this course, your own verdict. 
• Explain the paper in your own words. Do not read it aloud. 
• Upload your slides to CANVAS before the seminar starts. 
5. Attendance 
• Attend the whole seminar, not only your own slot. 
• A member who is absent fails. The seminar is held once. There is no resit, no later date and no alternative task. 
• Pass and fail are decided individually. If one member is absent, that member fails and the rest of the group is 
assessed normally. 
6. Deadlines 
• Recordings uploaded — 13-Oct , 23:59 
• Slides uploaded — 13 oct , 8:00 AM 
• Seminar — 13 oct 2026 from 14:45 to 18:00 

Grading 

The seminar is graded Pass or Fail. 

Result Requirements 
Pass All of the following: 
• The recording was submitted by the deadline, with the member's face visible. 
• The member attended the seminar and spoke during the presentation. 
• The paper is explained accurately: what problem it addresses, what data and method 
it uses, and what it found. 
• The group says what the paper does well and where it is weak, for example the limits 
of the dataset, missing baselines, metrics that flatter the result, or an evaluation the 
authors did not carry out. 
• The connection to this course is made explicit. 
• Questions from the audience are answered, and the member shows knowledge of the 
paper beyond their own section. 
• The presentation keeps within ten to twelve minutes and uses ten to ten. fifteen 
slides. 
Fail Any of the following: 
• The member is absent from the seminar. 
• The required recording was not submitted by the deadline. 
• The member does not speak during the presentation. 
• The presentation shows that the paper was not read or not understood. 
• The group runs out of time before every member has spoken. In this case the whole 
group fails, including the members who already presented. 

The seminar is held once.
